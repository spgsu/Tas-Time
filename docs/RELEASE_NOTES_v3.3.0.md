# Tas-Time 3.3.0

Routine work, small steps and an end-of-day review.

## What changed

- **Repeating tasks.** In a task editor, set **Repeats** to every N days, weeks or months. When you mark the task done, the next one is created with its dates moved on from the task's own schedule. Finishing early or late keeps the rhythm, and occurrences already in the past are skipped. Only the next occurrence exists at a time, so Plan and Week stay uncluttered.
- **Checklists.** Add small steps inside a task. They show as *2/5 steps*, never block completing the task, and start unticked on a repeating task's next occurrence.
- **Finish the day.** A new button in Today shows what you completed. For each unfinished task you choose **Tomorrow**, **Unplan** or **Leave**, and you can update the resume notes of the jobs you worked on. Nothing moves until you confirm, forecast dates never change, and one undo reverses the whole review.
- **Backups.** The browser keeps the last 10 versions of each book: one when you open it, then at most every 15 minutes while you work. Find them under **⋯ → Backups…**, where you can **Restore** (undoable) or **Download** them. They follow the recovery setting (now **Recovery copy & backups**): turning it off deletes them.

## Upgrading

No change to the book format (still format 4). Existing books open unchanged. Older 3.2 apps can still open books saved by 3.3, but they won't create the next occurrence of a repeating task.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** extract Tas-Time-Windows.zip and run Open Tas-Time.cmd.
- **macOS:** extract Tas-Time-macOS.zip; first launch, right-click Open Tas-Time.command and choose Open.
- **Single HTML:** open Tas-Time.html in Edge or Chrome.
